Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. Information We Access

When you sign in with Google, AMiR may access basic Google account information required to identify the signed-in user, such as your name and email address.

AMiR may also request access to Google Sheets and Google Drive to store and synchronize the application's school stor records.

2. Information Stored by AMiR

AMiR may store the following information:

  • Teacher's name
  • Date and time of item collection
  • Class
  • Activity or reason for collection
  • Record notes
  • Stor inventory information
  • Application record timestamps
  • Google account information required for authentication

AMiR does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or entered into AMiR is used only to:

  • Record and manage PJ stor activities
  • Manage stor inventory records
  • Synchronize records between authorized devices
  • Generate reports for school record-keeping purposes
  • Identify the Google account currently signed in to the application

AMiR does not sell or rent user information to third parties.

4. Google Drive and Google Sheets

AMiR uses Google Drive and Google Sheets to store and synchronize school stor data.

Access to these services is performed through Google's authentication and authorization systems. The school data remains within the Google account and Google Sheet configured for the AMiR workspace.

Users should only grant AMiR access when they understand and agree to the requested permissions.

5. Data Sharing

AMiR does not sell user information or share it with advertising companies.

School stor records may be accessible to users who have been granted access to the Google Sheet used by the school's AMiR workspace.

6. Data Security

AMiR uses Google authentication and Google-provided authorization mechanisms when accessing Google services.

Access tokens used by the application are handled for authentication and synchronization purposes and are not intentionally stored as permanent credentials by AMiR.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

School records stored in Google Sheets remain there until they are edited or deleted by an authorized user.

Users can delete records through the application where supported. Data stored in Google Drive or Google Sheets may also be managed directly by users with appropriate access.

Signing out of AMiR does not automatically delete school records from Google Drive or Google Sheets.

8. Third-Party Services

AMiR uses the following Google services:

  • Google Authentication
  • Google Drive
  • Google Sheets
  • Firebase Authentication

These services are subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AMiR's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or AMiR, please contact the application administrator through the school or application administration channel.
